Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for researchers, ML engineers, and data teams who take data privacy seriously.

Local-first privacy

Files are processed by the browser's File API. No server, no upload, no network request. GDPR, HIPAA, and NDA-compatible by design.

Keyboard-first speed

Press 1–9 to apply labels, arrow keys to navigate, Ctrl+Z to undo. Annotate hundreds of rows per hour without touching a mouse.

Multiple export formats

Export as CSV, JSON, or JSONL. JSONL is the standard for LLM fine-tuning — import directly into OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and Llama pipelines.

Fully customisable labels

Add, rename, or remove labels at any time. Default sentiment labels included, or define your own taxonomy for any classification task.

Session save & restore

Save your session to browser storage. Resume exactly where you left off — across page reloads, without any server-side persistence.

Progress tracking

A live progress bar shows annotated vs. remaining rows. Review all labels in context before exporting your completed dataset.

Up and running in 60 seconds

No installation. No sign-up. Open the page and annotate.

1

Upload your CSV

Drag and drop any UTF-8 CSV file onto the annotation tool. Or try one of the sample datasets to explore the tool first.

2

Select column & configure labels

Open Settings, pick which column to annotate, and add any custom labels your task requires. Default labels are ready to go immediately.

3

Annotate at speed

Press number keys 1–9 to label rows. Arrow keys to navigate. Ctrl+Z to undo. Your session autosaves every 5 seconds.

4

Export and use your data

Download as CSV, JSON, or JSONL when done. Files go straight to your Downloads folder. Nothing stays on any server.
