Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the individual version free forever?

Because EdgeLabel runs entirely on the client's device using HTML5 Web Workers and in-browser memory, Cueligence incurs zero server compute costs when individuals use the app. This allows us to offer an uncompromised, ad-free tool for open research.

Can small businesses run EdgeLabel completely offline?

Yes. EdgeLabel is a compliant Progressive Web App (PWA). Once cached, annotators can disconnect Wi-Fi entirely and label sensitive CSVs, patient records, or proprietary imagery in total isolation.

How does the Team Licence work?

Team licences allow managers to generate shared taxonomy codebooks, manage multi-annotator blinded batches, and verify regulatory audit trails for regulatory filings (FDA, EMA, SOC2).

How do we purchase or request an enterprise pilot?

Submit an inquiry on our Contact Page. Our solutions team responds within 24 hours to arrange an air-gapped container pilot or managed labelling evaluation.
