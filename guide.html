Client-Side Object Detection Labeling, YOLO & COCO Dataset Generation

The term Computer Vision often obscures the practical day-to-day workflow required to train vision models. EdgeVision is specifically designed for Image Annotation: the process of identifying objects in photos, enclosing them in tight 2D bounding boxes, categorizing them, and generating standardized training manifests.

1. Multi-Class Palette

Define custom classes (e.g. Person, Vehicle, Cyclist, Defect, Packaging) with distinct color swatches. Select classes using keys 1 to 9.

2. Interactive Canvas

Click and drag directly over the image to create a rectangle. Use corner anchor handles to adjust boundaries. Drag inside a box to translate its position.

3. YOLO & COCO Exporters

Download a fully structured YOLO .zip with normalized coordinate text files and dataset.yaml, or a standardized COCO .json file for Detectron2 and MMDetection.

Regardless of whether an image is $1920\times1080$ or $4000\times3000$, bounding box coordinates scale proportionally for model training.

Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA) & Adjudication

Statistical Concordance with Cohen's Kappa (κ) and Conflict Resolution

How do you know whether human labeling is objective and reliable? Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA) quantifies the degree of concordance between two or more independent raters evaluating the identical dataset. High agreement confirms that your annotation guidelines are clear and unambiguous.

The Math Behind Cohen's Kappa

Raw agreement percentage can be misleading because two annotators might agree simply by random coincidence (especially in imbalanced datasets). Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$) accounts for chance agreement:

κ = (Po − Pe) / (1 − Pe)
Po (Observed Agreement): The proportion of items where both annotators agreed on the exact category.
Pe (Chance Agreement): The probability that agreement occurred by pure chance given the marginal distributions.

Interpreting Your Kappa Score:

> 0.80 Almost Perfect
0.61 – 0.80 Substantial
0.41 – 0.60 Moderate
0.21 – 0.40 Fair Agreement
≤ 0.20 Poor / Unreliable

How to Adjudicate Disagreements

  1. Step 1: In the annotation tool, click Agreement & Adjudication (IAA) from the toolbar.
  2. Step 2: Select the secondary annotation CSV to compare against your current workspace.
  3. Step 3: Toggle the "Disagreements Only" filter. You will see side-by-side chips highlighting where Annotator A and Annotator B diverged.
  4. Step 4: Click the preferred label button to adopt it, or input a consensus resolution. The final unified file is exported as the gold-standard dataset.

Dataset Quality Assurance & Health Scanner

Automated Inconsistency, Data Leakage, and Imbalance Detection

Training an AI model on flawed datasets leads to silent evaluation failure. EdgeLabel's embedded Dataset Health Scanner audits your dataset in real time against five critical machine learning data quality rules:

1. Label Contradictions

Detects instances where identical input sentences were assigned conflicting labels (e.g., Row 14 is "Positive" while Row 204 is "Negative").

2. Redundant Duplicate Rows

Identifies repeated rows that risk leaking between training and validation splits, giving you falsely optimistic evaluation scores.

3. Severe Class Imbalance

Computes Shannon Entropy across classes. Warns when minority categories constitute less than 5% of samples, preventing classifier collapse.

4. Text Length Outliers

Flags anomalies such as empty/single-character strings or abnormally lengthy text blobs that will exceed token limits or indicate corrupted scraping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does EdgeLabel ever upload my data to the cloud?

No. All file reading, token extraction, bounding box computation, and dataset serialization happens locally in the browser sandbox. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi or run EdgeLabel in an air-gapped cleanroom and it will continue to function fully.

Who develops and maintains EdgeLabel?

EdgeLabel is built by Cueligence (formerly Clue Analytics), an AI and machine learning engineering practice that creates specialized, privacy-first software for enterprise and scientific teams.

Can I annotate both text and images in the same session?

Yes. EdgeLabel (Text & NER) and EdgeVision (Image Annotation) operate as specialized tools within the unified Cueligence suite. You can switch between tools using the top suite switcher at any time.

How do I preserve my work across browser restarts?

EdgeLabel automatically autosaves your progress to local storage every 5 seconds. When you return to the page on the same browser, your annotated rows and class schemes will restore seamlessly.
