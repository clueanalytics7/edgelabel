About EdgeLabel

Built by Clue Analytics

What we built and why

EdgeLabel is a data annotation and translation platform that runs entirely inside your browser. There is no server, no account, and no upload. Your dataset files are processed locally using the browser's built-in File API and never leave your device.

We built it because most annotation tools are cloud-first — useful for large teams, but a poor fit for researchers, independent data scientists, and small teams working with sensitive or proprietary data who need a credible privacy guarantee, not just a policy checkbox.

Who it's for

  • →NLP researchers who need to label text datasets for classification, sentiment, or intent detection without sending data to a third-party service.
  • →AI/ML teams preparing fine-tuning datasets for language models, including JSONL export ready for OpenAI, Anthropic, and Mistral formats.
  • →Localisation teams translating datasets row by row with structured CSV output, no separate translation software required.
  • →Clinical and legal professionals who cannot share patient or client data with cloud services due to regulatory or contractual requirements.

The privacy commitment

"Local-first" is not a marketing phrase here — it is a technical constraint we deliberately designed around. The annotation and translation tools have no network calls, no server, and no third-party data pipeline. Sessions are stored in your browser's localStorage, visible only to you, and exports go directly to your Downloads folder.

Optional Google Analytics (anonymised page-view statistics only) is available but is disabled by default and only activates when you explicitly accept the consent banner. Declining it has no effect on product functionality.
