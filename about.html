About EdgeLabel

A data annotation platform built on a single principle: your data is yours.

What we built and why

EdgeLabel is a data annotation and translation platform that runs entirely inside your browser. There is no server, no account, and no upload. Your dataset files are processed locally using the browser's built-in File API and never leave your device.

We built it because most annotation tools are cloud-first — useful for large teams, but a poor fit for researchers, independent data scientists, and small teams working with sensitive or proprietary data who need a credible privacy guarantee, not just a policy checkbox.

Who it's for

NLP Researchers

Label text datasets for classification, sentiment, or intent detection without sending data to a third-party service.

AI / ML Teams

Prepare fine-tuning datasets for language models with JSONL export ready for OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and Llama pipelines.

Localisation Teams

Translate datasets row by row with structured CSV output. No separate translation software required.

Clinical & Legal Professionals

Annotate data that cannot leave your device under GDPR, HIPAA, or NDA. Zero uploads, zero exposure.

The privacy commitment

"Local-first" is a technical constraint, not a marketing phrase. The annotation and translation tools have no network calls, no server, and no third-party data pipeline. Sessions are stored in your browser's localStorage, visible only to you, and exports go directly to your Downloads folder.

Optional Google Analytics (anonymised page-view statistics only) is disabled by default and only activates when you explicitly accept the consent banner. Declining it has no effect on product functionality.
