Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

The core promise: your dataset files are processed entirely in your browser using the File API. They are never uploaded, never transmitted, and never stored on any server.

Data you upload

EdgeLabel reads CSV files using the browser's built-in FileReader API. Files are processed in-memory in your browser. No file contents are ever sent to our servers or any third party. Annotation sessions are stored in your browser's localStorage, which is only accessible to you in that browser.

Google Analytics (opt-in only)

We offer optional Google Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated page-view statistics to help us understand how the product is used. This is disabled by default. It is only activated if you explicitly click Accept on the consent banner. The GA script is never loaded from the page head — it is injected dynamically after your consent. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage.

Cookies

We set one localStorage key (privacyConsent) to remember your analytics preference. No tracking cookies are set without your consent.

Personal information

We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information. There are no user accounts and no registration required to use EdgeLabel.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log page requests (IP address, browser, referrer) per their own Privacy Statement. EdgeLabel itself does not use any other third-party services.
